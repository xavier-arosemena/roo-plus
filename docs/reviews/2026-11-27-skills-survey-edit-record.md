This file holds the material that was removed from the public report on editorial review, plus the process record behind it. The reasoning: a research report is a description of a corpus, and a reader arriving at it has no access to its earlier drafts. Corrections belong in the record, not in the report — otherwise the report reads as a changelog of its own writing, and every correction costs the reader attention that the finding did not ask for.

## 3. Residual risks carried into publication

Stated in the report's header when it published, and repeated here:

1. **Token figures are characters ÷ 4.** The divisor is the rule of thumb in OpenAI's tokenizer documentation, now cited in the report's §5.2 and §10.3, and it was never validated against a tokenizer for _this_ text, which is identifier-dense (product names, environment variables, URLs) and runs closer to 3–3.5 characters per token — so dividing by four very likely **understates** every token figure here. Validating it means tokenizing the 2,044 measurable descriptions with a target tokenizer and publishing both the ratio and the totals.
2. **Licence observations are quotation, not advice.** Each is a statement about what a file says at a pinned commit, with path and SHA. They are not legal advice, and a qualified read remains the right step before anyone acts on them.

Neither is a defect in the data. Both are properties of a report that measured what it could and said where it stopped.

Two defects were introduced by this pass and caught before the re-publish, both by checking the rewritten sentence against the dataset rather than against prose:

1. §4.1's new paragraph ended "the specification defines none of them" while the §4.4 heading still said "one of which the specification defines". The heading was wrong and was changed.
2. §5.2 said "Thirteen files exceed the 1,024-character limit … twelve do once the quotations are stripped — twelve in one design catalogue, one in a vendor catalogue", which sums to thirteen twice. A recomputation over the inventory settles it: **13** rows exceed 1,024 under the raw-scalar convention, split **12 MengTo + 1 microsoft**; the "twelve after stripping" clause could not be reproduced and was dropped.

The same recomputation re-confirmed the figures the pass did not touch: 610 rows carry a sentinel length of 1 or 2 (264 + 346), leaving `n = 2,044`, mean 226.7, median 144.5, maximum 1,691 — the numbers printed in §5.2.
