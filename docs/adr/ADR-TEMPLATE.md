## Context

What is the issue we are facing? Why is a decision needed **now**?

Describe the forces at play: the current behavior, the pain or risk it causes,
the constraints (upstream fork alignment, packaging, security, backwards
compatibility), and the options that were considered. A good Context section
makes the decision legible to someone reading the ADR a year later.

## Decision

What did we decide to do? State it as one concrete, testable decision.

If alternatives were considered, list them here and explain why each was
**rejected** — this is what turns a "what we did" into a record of _why_.

## Migration Strategy (optional)

If the decision lands incrementally, describe the phases/milestones here (e.g.
schema-first, ratchet-gated migration, same-PR rule). Delete this section when
it is not needed.

## Consequences

### Positive

- What becomes easier, safer, or faster because of this decision?

### Negative

- What did we give up, what is now harder, or what debt did we accept?

### Neutral

- What changes for users/maintainers with no clear positive or negative?
